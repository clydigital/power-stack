Tech Sells Off as Treasuries Rally: Where I'd Put Cash

The Nasdaq fell 2.6% to 26,008.86 on 22 October, while the S&P 500 lost 1.2% and the Dow finished slightly higher. The difference matters: investors were not selling everything, but reducing exposure to expensive technology and AI infrastructure.

Treasury prices rose as the ten-year yield fell below 5% intraday. TLT gained 1.87% to $81.74 and IEF rose 1.08% to $91.20. Bond buying is consistent with a growth or financing scare, but persistently expensive oil could revive inflation pressure and make long-duration bonds volatile.

Power Stack can still buy. My first duration study is VGIT at $57.45 (lower interest-rate sensitivity), followed by IEF; TLT is a smaller tactical satellite. CF Industries at $118.56 is the first non-AI equity entry study on cash, valuation and nitrogen fundamentals. These are candidates for staged, limited entries, not unsized orders.

What Actually Changed

At Thursday's close, SPY fell 1.18% and QQQ fell 2.86%. Semiconductor ETF SMH fell 5.99%, while the equal-weight S&P ETF RSP rose 0.73%, consumer staples ETF XLP gained 3.31% and healthcare ETF XLV gained 1.32%. This is an important rotation rather than index-only panic.

The ten-year Treasury dipped under 5% while Meta fell 6.98%, Micron fell 6.26%, GE Vernova fell 10.64%, and Eaton fell 6.33%. These are 22 October completed US closing prices, not earnings estimates. Match the bond rally against HY/IG spreads before calling it broad credit contagion.

Brent/oil inflation and French sovereign risk remain material. A Treasury flight to safety can coexist with rising inflation expectations; do not infer an automatic Fed rate cut from lower nominal yields.

The Bond Decision — Buy Duration, but Choose It Carefully

VGIT closed at $57.45 with a Vanguard-reported duration of roughly 4.8–4.9 years and a 0.03% fee. IEF closed at $91.20 and provides longer 7–10-year exposure. Either can be an initial Treasury allocation; owning both is not true diversification because their main driver is US interest rates.

TLT closed at $81.74 after a 1.87% jump. iShares reports an effective duration of 14.79 years and a 30-day SEC yield of 5.41% as of 16 October. A 0.50 percentage-point parallel yield increase could imply roughly 7.4% mark-to-market price loss before coupons and convexity. Treat TLT as a small satellite, not safe cash.

SGOV remains suitable for USD liquidity and dry powder. An individual Treasury maturing on a chosen date can make more sense if the actual objective is guaranteed principal at maturity, rather than a mark-to-market bond-price trade. MYR/USD exposure, taxes and trading costs still matter.

Where the Real Earnings Are

TSMC's 15 October Q3 issuer report showed $46.72bn US-dollar revenue, 66.4% gross margin, 58.7% operating margin and Q4 revenue guidance of $52.3–54.3bn. The stock still fell 4.52% on 22 October. That is valuation and factor repricing to investigate, not proof that chip orders disappeared.

Meta's Q3 revenue reached $64.60bn and operating cash flow $32.79bn, while company-wide free cash flow was negative $2.12bn after massive capex. Its 2026 capex range is $135–145bn, narrowed from $130–145bn; a large legal charge also distorted operating earnings. Credit-sensitive customers are more vulnerable than cash-rich hyperscalers.

CF Industries generated $1.34bn H1 net earnings and $1.82bn trailing free cash flow according to the issuer. A provisional same-listed vendor screen gives 8.44x trailing earnings and 9.28x trailing price/free cash flow. The next test is North American natural-gas cost and the 4 November Q3 release, not simply whether oil rises.

What I Would Buy and Sell

BUY CANDIDATES: a staged VGIT or IEF starter; SGOV for uncommitted cash; a smaller TLT position only if long-end yields and loss budget justify it; CF as the first cash-producing non-AI equity research purchase after a fresh Friday quote.

HOLD or WAIT: XOM as existing core energy; TTWO pending launch evidence; TSM pending tech breadth stabilisation; GEV until 28 October Q3; NEM until its Q3 release is read; GRAB pending Atome/credit and normalized cash. Do not blindly chase a one-day defensive sector rally.

SELL/TRIM REVIEWS: DAYANG is the highest priority for capital recycling because operating results weakened; TSLA merits a trim review after earnings and the 22 October fall; consider reducing YTLPOWR only if verified parent-plus-warrant exposure breaches the user's risk cap.
