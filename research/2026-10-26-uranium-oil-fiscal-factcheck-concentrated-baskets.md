## Verifications against supplied In It to Win It / Lobo Tiggre interview transcript
1. **Supported: Uranium spot prices held up while miner equities declined.** Cameco's September month-end uranium spot price $89.63/lb and term indicator $96.50/lb; late October comparison ~$90/lb but quotes are assessed/benchmark data, not liquid exchange settlement. URNM closed $41.79 on Oct 23, with third-party history down ~17.44% over one month. Those facts do not establish intrinsic equity undervaluation.
2. **Supported with a catch: physical trust trades below estimated NAV.** Third-party Oct 23 TSX U.U discount ~14.08% with NAV dated Oct 22. Sprott's Oct 9 own page showed ~11.62%. The discount changes daily. Sprott's trust is closed end, NAV can decline, and the trust has no normal redemption mechanism permitting investors to close the gap on demand. Sprott indicated it chose not to implement a redemption feature.
3. **Supported structurally, not as a near-term timing signal:** World Nuclear Association 2025 report reference scenario projects reactor uranium requirements rising from ~68,920 tU in 2025 to over 150,000 tU in 2040. Future new capacity, contracts, inventory and mines have multi-year lags; spot price does not flow immediately into every miner's earnings.
4. **Overstated: 'one terawatt more of nuclear power contracted by Alphabet and Meta yesterday.'** Google confirmed 890 MW in its Oct 6 Constellation PPA, and Meta announced up to 6.6 GW of nuclear projects in Jan 2026. Neither fact validates 1 TW (1,000 GW) contracted in one day. Separate power capacity (MW/GW) from energy (MWh/TWh) and fuel requirements.
5. **Incorrect: Fukushima was not a nuclear accident because of the tsunami.** IAEA classified Fukushima Daiichi as a major INES level 7 accident involving radiological release; the tsunami was the initiating hazard, not grounds to dismiss the event.
6. **Partly supported: fiscal dominance.** CBO estimated a ~$2.0tr US fiscal-year 2026 federal deficit. FRED H.6 reported US M2 ~$23.343tr (Aug 2026). These do NOT mathematically require equities/commodities to increase and do not make 25–50bp rate changes meaningless. The price-level path depends on real supply, demand, interest rates, velocity and policy; real yields and term premium can fall or rise.
7. **Contradictory alternative oil base case:** Lobo's 80–90/bbl higher-for-longer is a speculative investment thesis. EIA October 6 STEO projected Brent ~$87/bbl in 2Q27 and ~$74/bbl in 4Q27 as bypass capacity and regional supply recover. War escalation could instead keep prices higher.
8. **Broker access matters:** Rakuten Trade advertises Bursa, NYSE/Nasdaq and HKEX, not direct TSX. Therefore TSX Sprott Physical Uranium Trust U.U / U.UN should be treated as access-unverified; US-listed URNM and CCJ are candidates only after exact order ticket confirms availability. URNM owns both CCJ (~20% Oct 2) and Sprott physical trust (~16% Oct 2), so combining them repeats exposure. Direct U.U differs fundamentally from URNM: physical price+discount versus producer equity, operating and funding risk.
