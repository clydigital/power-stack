## Hedge limitations
- MYR cash protects local nominal liquidity, not inflation-adjusted purchasing power.
- SGOV has low US rate duration but carries USD/MYR and distribution/tax risk.
- VGIT may lose value if inflation or term premium lifts yields; it is not a reliable hedge against every oil shock.
- Gold may diversify monetary and geopolitical stress but can fall when real yields rise.
- CF, ECVT and IVN are correlated to industrial activity, commodity supply and risk appetite, not three independent hedges.
- No shorts, leverage or options are assumed. Diversification limits risk but does not ensure positive returns.
