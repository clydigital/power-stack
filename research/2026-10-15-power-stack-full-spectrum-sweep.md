### Agriculture: re-underwrite the entire value chain, not generic food inflation

USDA's September 2026 farm-sector forecast expects fertilizer/lime/soil-conditioner expenses **+15.3%** and fuel/oil spending **+28.8%**, as real net farm income declines **5.5%** versus 2025. This may benefit a cost-advantaged fertilizer producer but also makes farmers more price-sensitive. [USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-economy/farm-sector-income-finances/farm-sector-income-forecast). USDA's 30 September grain-stock data showed corn stocks **+35%**, soybean stocks **−3%**, and wheat stocks **−13%** year on year: the inventory picture does not justify a blanket long-every-crop trade. [USDA NASS](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Newsroom/2026/09-30-2026.php).

[14 October DTN retail survey](https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/crops/article/2026/10/14/uan32-leads-fertilizer-prices-higher) put urea near **$672/ton (+12% YoY)** and UAN32 near **$485/ton (+6% month-on-month)**. These are sampled farm-input retail prices, not CF or NTR realised producer selling prices.

- **CF — highest-priority agriculture study.** Underwrite urea/ammonia selling price, US natural gas feedstock, plant utilisation, global disrupted supply, transport, farmer demand and free cash flow. Strong urea prices without a persistent spread over operating costs are insufficient.
- **NTR — comparison, not equivalent exposure.** Potash volumes/price and retail network economics differ from CF nitrogen. Verify grower affordability, capex and credit, not simply headline fertilizer inflation.
- **CTVA vs VYLR — thesis reset.** Corteva's October split means prior seed/genetics arguments no longer belong indiscriminately to the remaining CTVA business. Rebuild pure-play crop protection and spun-off seed/genetics economics separately before scoring.
- **BG — downstream crop/processing comparator.** Focus on merchandising basis, crush margins and working capital. More expensive farm inputs do not automatically raise merchandiser earnings.
- **VMI / XYL / MWA — water scarcity.** Favour funded municipal/industrial infrastructure over discretionary, debt-financed farm irrigation. MWA is new and *unscored*.
- **MOS — cost trap.** Rising phosphate price can be offset by expensive sulphur/sulphuric-acid feedstock and freight; wait for actual margins.
- **Beef/cattle, wheat, corn, soy, rubber, sugar:** keep as background screens, not active equity promotions. Update primary herd, inventory, export, weather, CFTC positioning and processing margin data before changing exposure. Older August scarcity overlays are historical, not live proof.

### Energy: product spreads matter more than a generic crude headline

The [IEA October Oil Market Report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-october-2026) reports September global gasoil/diesel exports about 700,000 bpd below the previous year and warns product tightness could persist into 2027 even as Gulf crude exports recovered. Separate crude availability, finished diesel/jet/LPG output, refining economics and the logistics/freight premium. **VLO and STNG** are direct priority lenses; MPC/PSX are peer checks. **LNG** underwrite Cheniere's contracted cargoes and distributable cash rather than spot-gas narratives.
