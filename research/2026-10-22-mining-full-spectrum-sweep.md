### A. The decisive change to the copper thesis

The **ICSG 14 October 2026** update forecasts **refined copper surpluses** of **104,000 tonnes in 2026** and **368,000 tonnes in 2027**, with China unreported-stock uncertainty; this **replaces** April's 96,000/377,000-tonne projections. Mine-concentrate constraints can co-exist with surplus refined metal. As of 21 October LME copper was around **$14,320/tonne** with visible inventories rising. At this price, miner margins can be exceptional while the next marginal buyer has poor asymmetry. ([ICSG 14 Oct primary announcement](https://icsg.org/download/2026-10-14-press-release-icsg-copper-market-forecast-2026-2027/); [Japanese Mining Foundation reproduction of ICSG](https://j-mining-pf.jp/icsg202610/); [SMM 20 Oct](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104166605-icsg-global-refined-copper-surplus-to-reach-368000-t-in-2027)).

**SCCO**: official 20 October Q3 **38.4% net margin** vs 32.8% last year, **$2.224bn CFO** and **$516m capex**, though copper output fell. **$0.09/lb net-of-byproduct cash cost is not a timeless mine cost**: metal by-product prices drove the dramatic cost drop. Finnhub 21 October US close **$198.07**, indicative same-listing TTM **28.7x earnings and 31.8x price/FCF** — very good earnings, not obvious value. [Issuer SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001838/000110465926117588/scco-ex99d1.htm).

**FCX**: recent operational update has **~830m lb Q3 copper** with Grasberg's early-October mill near **67% normal throughput**; quantify 2027 restart costs before paying for copper recovery. Finnhub 21 Oct **$68.90**, preliminary **33.4x TTM earnings and ~39.8x trailing price/FCF**. Q3 earnings expected **27 October**. [Issuer update](https://investors.fcx.com/investors/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Freeport-Provides-Third-Quarter-2026-Operational-Update/default.aspx).

**RIO**: Oyu Tolgoi copper output **+29% YTD**, ~450kt annualised rate in Q3. Positive operational differentiation versus BHP's FY27 Q1 copper **-19%**, but iron-ore exposure and Simandou ramp may dominate equity cash returns. ([Rio 14 Oct](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2026/rio-tinto-releases-third-quarter-2026-production-results); [BHP 20 Oct](https://www.bhp.com/news/media-centre/releases/2026/10/bhp-operational-review-for-the-quarter-ended-30-september-2026)).
