## Why the adjacent layer
If GPU/data-centre growth broadens, the investable economic winners need not be chip designers or levered GPU cloud operators. Differentiated providers can supply power distribution, thermal management, reliable industrial water, medical imaging, biological instruments and robotic procedures. These companies still carry AI-cycle and interest-rate sensitivity; sector labels alone do not prove diversification.

## Active study — 3 issuer-grounded additions

### VRT — cooling, power and thermal management
- **Fact (July 29, 2026 SEC/issuer):** Q2 sales $3.274bn, +24% y/y. Operating profit +44%; adjusted operating margin 22.6% (+410bp y/y). Adjusted FCF $925m, above the prior-year run-rate. Company raised FY2026 guidance.
- **Investment mechanism:** densifying racks increase liquid cooling, power continuity and service needs. Revenue/cash conversion already observable, unlike speculative AI-service monetisation.
- **Contradictions:** stronger order book can inflate valuation; project timings and new capacity can move quarterly revenue.
- **Next test:** latest orders and backlog vs fulfilment, cash FCF definition, valuation, capex intensity, customer concentration and a priced retest.
- **Verdict:** Research only, not an automatic buy.
- **Issuer evidence:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1674101/000162828026050323/q22026exhibit991vrt07292026.htm

### XYL — water and industrial reliability
- **Fact (2026 Q2 issuer results):** Q2 orders ~$3.1bn, +42% y/y, versus revenue ~$2.3bn, +2%. Adjusted EBITDA margin 23.3% (+150bp). Management explicitly cited semiconductor and power-related water needs.
- **Investment mechanism:** fabs, power plants and industrial production require water treatment, quality and uptime, but municipalities remain a separate demand base.
- **Contradiction:** orders are not revenue; claimed AI exposure may be materially smaller than municipal or industrial replacement cycles.
- **Next test:** convert orders to revenue and FCF, disaggregate AI-facing business, verify organic backlog and valuation.
- **Verdict:** Research only.
- **Issuer evidence:** https://www.xylem.com/en-us/resources/press-releases/xylem-reports-second-quarter-2026-results/

### GEHC — imaging, diagnostics and medical AI
- **Fact (July 29, 2026 issuer):** Q2 total revenue ~$5.3bn, +5.7% y/y; organic orders +11.1%; backlog $23.9bn; FCF $68m; debt $10.1bn; patient-care solutions operating segment under pressure. AI imaging is a product/tool story, not an established AI profit segment.
- **Fresh structural event (October 5, 2026 issuer):** definitive agreement to acquire SOFIE Biosciences for $945m cash, extending U.S. PET radiopharmaceutical manufacturing/distribution and FAPI-74 pipeline rights. Do not treat the acquisition as confirmed end-user AI profit.
- **Mechanism:** integrated imaging workflows, installed base and radiotracer access may strengthen diagnostics repeat revenue, but the purchase may strain returns on capital.
- **Next test:** next quarterly results, segment revenue, acquisition return, debt interest, AI-enabled software attach and regulated patient outcome validation.
- **Verdict:** Research only; healthcare diversification requires actual patient-market unit economics.
- **Issuer evidence:** https://www.gehealthcare.com/en-us/about/newsroom/press-releases/ge-healthcare-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results
- **Issuer M&A announcement:** https://investor.gehealthcare.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ge-healthcare-acquire-sofie-biosciences-establishing-final-mile
