## What's genuinely new

1. **SCCO Q3, 20 October:** Net margin **38.4%**, from **32.8%** a year earlier; quarterly net income **$1.802bn**, up **62.7%**. CFO **$2.224bn**. Copper output slipped, implying the earnings surge depends heavily on commodity-price realisation. Its net by-product-credit copper cash cost at $0.09/lb is **not** a constant underlying cost: by-product metal prices are affecting this figure.
2. **BHP, 20 October:** FY27 Q1 copper production **down 19%**, against copper price realisation substantially above a year ago. Jansen potash Stage 1 **87%** complete; first production anticipated mid-2027. Both constrain simple extrapolation of booming copper prices into rising per-share profit.
3. **Rio Tinto, 14 October:** Oyu Tolgoi copper production **up 29% YTD**, running near a 450 kt annualised rate in the quarter. Iron ore and Simandou exposures may dampen pure copper bullishness.
4. **FCX, 2 October:** Q3 copper production ~**830m lb**; Grasberg mill throughput ~**67%** of normalised pre-incident rates. FCX expects to approach full capacity in late 2027. This is an execution/recovery thesis as well as a copper-price thesis.
5. **AEM, Q2 results 29 July:** Quarterly FCF **$1.335bn**; royalties, labour and energy costs up. Q3 results **28 October** after US market close.
6. **WPM, Q2 results 6 August:** Quarterly revenue **$929m** and operating cash flow **$650m**. Streaming has different mine-operation economics but retains counterparty, concentration, pricing and funding risks. Q3 **5 November** after US close.
7. **World Gold Council, 24 August:** Q1 average global gold-miner AISC **$1,785/oz**, up **16% YoY**, despite record industry margin dollars. Oil shock hurts miners via diesel, freight, royalties and supplies.
8. **Reuters, 22 October:** Spot gold around **$4,082/oz** amid oil-led inflation and tighter-rate expectations. **Reuters, 21 October:** Copper about **$14,319.50/t** on the LME, with visible inventory build. Structural copper demand does not prove an attractive purchase at today's commodity prices.
9. **Teck, 21 October:** Highland Valley Copper strike notice creates company-specific execution uncertainty; do not treat the disrupted capacity as a guaranteed bullish investment catalyst.

Mining has optionality against AI/long-duration concentration, but the inflation response creates adverse effects on gold and production cost. Higher copper also lifts GEV/ETN/SCGBHD input costs unless passed through. Preserve cash-flow distinctions, source priority and the existing 12-investigation cap.
