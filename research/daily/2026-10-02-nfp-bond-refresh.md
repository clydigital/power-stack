# Power Stack — September NFP / Treasury Duration Refresh

**Date:** 2 October 2026  
**Scope:** Bond watch + portfolio macro overlay. No company fundamental scores changed.

## Executive read

September payrolls printed **+29k versus ~+90k expected**. Unemployment rose to **4.2%**, participation improved to **61.8%**, average hourly earnings slowed to **+0.1% m/m / +3.0% y/y**, and July/August were revised down by a combined **60k**.

That combination is enough to seriously weaken the case for an October Fed hike. It is **not** enough to say the whole 2026 hike cycle is dead: Reuters still had December pricing near **90%** after the report.

## What happened in bonds

The immediate reaction was textbook duration relief. The US 2Y fell toward **4.72%** and the 10Y toward **5.18%**. Oil also fell more than 3% intraday, which helps the near-term inflation read.

The catch is the long end. TLT does not only care about the next Fed meeting. It also cares about 20Y/30Y yields, real yields, inflation expectations, Treasury supply and term premium.

## TLT read

**Status: TACTICAL STARTER / WATCH — not full-size.**

TLT's setup is finally interesting because the carry and convexity are both high. iShares reported a **5.49% 30-day SEC yield**, **5.65% portfolio YTM** and **14.74-year effective duration** on 29 September.

That duration means a 25 bp parallel drop in long yields is roughly a **+3.7%** price impulse before convexity and carry. A 25 bp rise works the other way.

Short interest is also elevated: **107.8m shares / 19.2% of float** as of 15 September. That can accelerate a rally if yields break lower, but it is not the reason to own the trade.

### Confirmation

- 10Y stops making fresh highs and sustains a move below 5.0%.
- 20Y/30Y rally too, rather than only the front end.
- Real yields stop rising.
- Crude **and** diesel/product inflation keep cooling.
- TLT holds the Oct 1 low area and starts building a base.

### Invalidation

- 10Y retakes the recent ~5.34% high and holds.
- 20Y/30Y make fresh highs.
- TLT loses the Oct 1 low area.
- Diesel/product inflation reaccelerates and breakevens rise.

## Portfolio read

Lower yields improve the discount-rate backdrop for duration-heavy holdings/candidates such as **TSLA, YTLPOWR and premium AI/power names**.

Falling crude is the opposite for the energy sleeve: **XOM, MISC, HIBISCS and DAYANG** lose some near-term macro tailwind if oil/product prices continue to normalize. That is a macro overlay only; it does not justify changing company scores by itself.

## Data-quality caveat

Reuters noted that late-Labor-Day seasonal adjustment effects may have depressed September payrolls. There is no need to assume the headline is either perfectly clean or politically manipulated. The better test is whether the story survives revisions and agrees with claims, household employment, wages, hours and the next report.
