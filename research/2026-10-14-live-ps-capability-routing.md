# Power Stack ← Live Desk: reusable research skills, apps and APIs
**Decision and integration note · 14 October 2026 · research-only**

## Executive decision
**Do not build another MacroPulse, Research Gap, Story/Regime reasoner, rate engine, API key store or Market Motion producer inside Power Stack.** Existing [Live Market Desk](https://github.com/clydigital/alchemy-live-market-desk) owns the underlying market facts, source health, contradiction handling and canonical narratives; PS owns investment translation (issuer cash flow, competitive economics, valuation, existing holdings, concentration, entries and watchlist decisions). Preserve separate owners so apparent corroboration does not come from looping one model's conclusions back into itself.

**Available ≠ healthy ≠ deployed ≠ licensed ≠ independently verified.** This note audits repository code and documented tooling, not Vercel secrets, exchange-data licences, active plugin connections, or authenticated production telemetry. Never copy shared keys into GitHub Pages, static JSON, browser JS or logs.

## Existing integration (not newly invented)
- The Power Stack [Live bridge](https://github.com/clydigital/power-stack/blob/main/scripts/sync-live-desk.mjs) already reads `GET /api/market-intelligence-snapshot` and requires `market-intelligence-snapshot/v1`. It emits `data/live-desk-canonical.json`, `power-stack-live-desk-canonical/2`, with Dossier ID/as-of, regime, monetary signals, source health, contradictions, research gaps, story/lens links, assets, stock radar and exact promoted immutable Motion where supplied.
- The committed PS snapshot checked on 14 October still has Dossier **d44eb5d4-1404-4d28-8f7d-a270963b7136**, `asOf 2026-10-12T22:11:23.968+00:00`, `sourceStatus=degraded_latest`, long-end rates but canonical HY/IG, MOVE and non-AI breadth confirmation unresolved. This is **a dated stored reading**, not proof of a fresh live response.
- `data/portfolio-live-overlay.json` and `data/power-sweep-latest.json` already conduct bounded holding/watchlist/theme investigations. The existing cap is 12 external investigations per sweep: 3 holdings, 4 watchlist, 3 themes and up to 3 China adjunct within the total. Reuse it rather than adding unlimited event probes.
- `data/ai-catalyst-gates.json` adds an **explicit source-linked October 15 TSMC event**, whose findings can enter current queues only after issuer evidence and security-price checks. Missing evidence means `UNKNOWN`, not favourable/negative.

## Skills worth borrowing, not cloning
1. **Public Equity Investing — thesis-tracker / catalyst-calendar / earnings-preview / earnings-deep-dive / portfolio-risk-management.** Use its falsifiable thesis pillars, original underwriting, missing-data gate, profit/cash sensitivities, valuation readiness, decision log and dated event confidence. These are analysis workflows, not an alternative web service.
2. **Data Analytics — validate-data / gather-business-context / product-business-analysis.** Apply consistent period, currency, sign, fiscal vs calendar quarter, unit normalization and stale-data checks. Avoid two scores for the same company from two sources.
3. **Live Research Gap / Regime / Dossier evidence sufficiency.** Borrow the *source classification* and conflict/contradiction practice: `VERIFIED` issuer fact, `ATTRIBUTED` reporting, `RESEARCH_LEAD` creator or opinion, `UNKNOWN` missing. Borrow policy but keep PS's issuer-security decision owner.
4. **OpenAlex research papers** (when available): verify methods and broader technology/power narratives. Papers cannot replace issuer earnings, live bond prices or EPS expectations.
5. **Quartr transcripts and SEC IR** (where callable): anchor capex guidance revision and management explanation to a specific speaker, period, date and company. Detect changed versus repeated claims.

## TSMC gate — what the October 15 result can and cannot tell us
[Issuer's Q3 page](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q3) schedules the conference for **15 Oct 2026, 14:00 Taiwan/Malaysia time**. Before release, the issuer's Q3 guidance is **$44.6–45.8B revenue, 65–67% gross margin and 56–58% operating margin**. Already published [Q3 revenue rose ~50% year-on-year](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/tsmcs-third-quarter-revenue-surges-50-yy-beating-market-forecast-2026-10-08/). [Reuters' expected profit](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/tsmc-expected-report-sixth-straight-quarter-record-profit-thanks-ai-boom-2026-10-12/) is a *pre-release estimate*, not the actual result.

**After release:** capture actual issuer profit, gross and operating margins, Q4 outlook, FY2026 capex changes, HPC/advanced packaging utilisation, management explanations, CFO/capex/FCF and exact market close. Compare guidance in original currency, units and periods first. Strong TSMC orders are evidence of hardware demand; they cannot establish OpenAI or Anthropic profitability or eliminate financing circularity.

**Adjudication routes:** (a) sustained orders + cash economics => constructive on physical suppliers, subject to entry value; (b) slowing capex + improving return economics => *healthy digestion*; (c) funding strain plus real order/earnings deterioration and spreading credit stress => escalation; (d) missing/contradictory evidence => no score change, Research Gap. Use the company-specific proof questions already present in `data/watchlist.json` for MU, GEV, ETN and AVGO, and preserve the existing 8-company homepage focus and 46 stock rankings.

## Deployment/gating and next extraction
- Use existing Live source health, Dossier exact IDs and verified as-of for any macro statement. If current `GET /api/market-intelligence-snapshot` fails or source is stale, record the error and postpone regime-sensitive upgrades; do **not** import a cached press narrative as fresh canonical Live.
- Research only the decision-changing issuer questions inside current PowerSweep capacity. No API-key or credential changes required for this design.
- Refresh PS data article, event card, watchlist or decision gates **only when actual evidence changes**; the pre-release gate must never be displayed as if the result has been adjudicated.
- Any change to company score, target, sizing or add/trim gate requires independent issuer proof **plus current traded price/valuation**. Report change/no change and traceable sources.
