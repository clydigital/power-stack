[FRED ICSA](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/ICSA); [Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-weekly-jobless-claims-near-57-year-lows-ce785cdedb89f523); [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/u-s-jobless-claims-return-to-downward-trend-9ba853fc).

**B. Meta AI-return causality cannot be isolated** from overall cash PP&E and lease spending, and $9.89bn legal expense cannot be interpreted as AI deterioration. The right conclusion is *very large capex outlays with cash generation but negative Q3 after-capex free cash flow*, not insolvency nor proved AI project NPV loss.

**C. Unsupported precision in prior visual risk meter:** red/yellow 90%/85%/70% bars were **illustrative subjective concern**, not calibrated tail-risk probabilities or a backtested regime indicator. Do not ingest bars numerically or automate trades from them.

**D. Cross-listed miner valuations:** Finnhub unsuffixed Canada/SA/Australia primary-exchange metric inputs can mismatch NYSE USD ADR quote; earlier dataset already flagged this. Even US primary-listed NEM/AA/SCCO/FCX trailing P/E and P/FCF do not prove undervaluation: cash at peak metal prices can exaggerate cheapness. No buy gate promoted.

**E. Oil and precious-metals quote times:** Reuters Brent +2.81% to $111.88 at 11:04 UTC and spot gold -1.9% to $4,060.37 at 11:34 UTC, not a matched event study.
