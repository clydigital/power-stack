## The actual financing chain
1. OpenAI/Anthropic agree to purchase compute. Hyperscalers/builders book future cloud business and fund GPU/network/grid construction.
2. Some major sponsors of the labs also supply their cloud/equipment. Capital can circulate through related parties while **ultimate paying-customer cash profit** remains much smaller than announced gross bookings.
3. [Nvidia's July 2026 10-Q, Note 7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm) lists **five direct customers making up approximately 70% of receivables**. This is not proof that two labs make up 70% of Nvidia revenue or that customers are delinquent.
4. [Oracle's August 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526389274/orcl-20260831.htm) lists **$664B RPO**. Backlog is not cash, profit, or a guarantee of timely execution; [customer-supplied or prepaid equipment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312526265848/orcl-ex99_1.htm) partly mitigates the funding gap.
5. [Microsoft FY2026 Q4](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4) **generated positive $19.6B FCF**. Do not repeat Eisman's blanket claim that hyperscaler FCF has vanished. The '70% of hyperscaler AI revenue from two labs' interview figure is not validated on comparable issuer denominators.
6. Open-weight Chinese model competition potentially compresses frontier LLM per-token pricing, but can also expand compute traffic. Safety-regulation-as-moat claims are Eisman's unverified motive attribution; exclude them from fundamental scores.

[GEV Q2 2026](https://www.gevernova.com/news/articles/ge-vernova-releases-second-quarter-2026-financial-results) showed a **$176B backlog**; [Eaton Q2](https://origin-edge.eaton.com/us/en-us/company/news-insights/news-releases/2026/eaton-reports-record-second-quarter-2026-results.html) showed **41% Electrical Americas rolling orders growth** and **43% Electrical-sector backlog growth YoY**. These figures establish commercial strength at the reported dates, not October 14 purchase prices.

## Three conditional paths
- **Base (current):** spending continues, broad index near highs, chip leadership weakens, labs require more outside cash. Strong contracted suppliers may outperform builders. Maintain selective research and conservative entries.
- **Bullish / healthy digestion:** third-party paid AI demand rises, inference gross margins/FCF improve, orders remain strong while capex growth slows, market breadth and semiconductor relative strength stabilise. A slowdown in new capex can be good if returns improve.
- **Bearish / break:** failed lab financing or worsening payments **plus** independently verified order reductions/deployment deferrals **plus** weaker guidance, utilisation and widening borrower credit spreads. Only the joint evidence makes a major-warning review warranted; do not call a bubble top from an interview or chart drawdown.
