The score is deliberately harsher than the main Power Stack conviction system. A great narrative cannot rescue weak cash flow, excessive leverage or an extreme valuation here.

Scores are cross-sector judgements, not mechanical screens. Property companies are judged with more weight on book value, core earnings and leverage; software and gaming receive more credit for recurring revenue and IP quality; cyclical energy is judged through-cycle rather than on a single oil-price quarter.

Important: this is not a buy/sell signal and does not include portfolio sizing, entry price timing, macro regime, technicals or hedge-fund positioning.
