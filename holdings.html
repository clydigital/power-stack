SCORING METHOD

Fundamental Score remains the slow-moving 0–100 anchor. Earnings, cash flow, balance sheet, business quality, growth and valuation are not altered by a hot theme or a friendly macro print.

Macro reuses the All Ideas stock-specific channel formula and is capped at ±1.00. Developing Themes reads the same live theme feed and only contributes when a holding is explicitly listed as Power Stack exposure or a research candidate.

Live Score = Fundamental + 5×Macro + 5×Themes. Each overlay can move the portfolio score by at most five points, so the business remains the anchor. Stale macro packets automatically contribute zero.
