WHY THIS EXISTS

Give the research a story without letting headlines own the thesis.

Yahoo Finance and Reuters are useful discovery surfaces for the things people are actually talking about: a Meta product launch, Nvidia capital return, a Tesla regulatory deadline, Micron earnings, or a new Hormuz negotiation headline. Power Stack keeps the hook, then asks what it changes in the bigger picture.

Guardrail: Market Motion is a narrative/discovery layer. A fresh headline can raise research priority, create a Hybrid hook or tighten an action gate, but it cannot change a company fundamental score until primary/company evidence supports that change.
